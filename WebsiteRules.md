The 5 Key Patterns for High-Converting AI Websites:

1.  **The Mirror Test:** Your first screen must act like a mirror for your visitor. It should clearly address their **pain points**, define **who it is for**, and provide a clear **promise of the outcome**. You must answer three questions: "Who is this for?", "What do I get?" (outcome, not just features), and "What do I do next?" (a clear Call to Action).
2.  **The Highlighter Rule:** Avoid being "highlighter happy" by over-using accent colors. Follow the **60-30-10 rule** used in interior design: 60% for the background, 30% for structure, and only 10% for your high-contrast accent color—reserved exclusively for your primary buttons and links to guide user action.
3.  **Front, Middle, Back:** This pattern prevents the common "flat" look of AI sites by creating visual depth. Think of it as a **theater stage**:
    - **Back:** The background (a photo or soft shape to set the mood).
    - **Middle:** Content (headline, text, cards).
    - **Front:** The primary interaction (your Call to Action button) sitting on top of everything else with a soft shadow to lift it off the page.
4.  **Motion with a Job:** Animations should never be just for show. Each animation must serve a **specific function**, such as displaying a product, pointing to the next step, or showing a result. Follow three rules: limit movement to one object per screen, ensure text is readable immediately without waiting for animations, and provide an "off switch" or respect user system-level motion settings.
5.  **The Free Sample:** Give visitors a taste of the value before asking for a commitment. Use tools like a quick **quiz**, a **price calculator**, or a **useful interactive tool** (like a room visualizer) to leverage the _IKEA Effect_—where people value results more when they help create them.

Important:
** Required color structure **

- Never use purle or violet prominently, unless explicitly asked for.
- If you override a components background color, you MUST override its text color to ensure proper contrast.
- Be sure to override text colors if you change a background color.
